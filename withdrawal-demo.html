Why a screenshot proves nothing

A screen like the one above is just HTML. Anyone can make one look "completed" with any address, amount, date and time. Never accept a screenshot as proof of payment.

How to check a real USDT (BEP20) payment

  1. Ask the sender for the transaction hash (starts with 0x, 66 characters).
  2. Open bscscan.com and paste the hash in the search box.
  3. Check that Status says Success.
  4. Under ERC-20/BEP-20 Tokens Transferred, check that the token is the real Binance-Peg BSC-USD (USDT), not a lookalike.
  5. Check that the To address is exactly your address. Compare all characters, not just the first and last few.
  6. Check the amount and the timestamp.
  7. Best of all: check your own wallet balance. If the money isn't there, it wasn't paid.
